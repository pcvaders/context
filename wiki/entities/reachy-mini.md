# Reachy Mini

> Expressive desktop robot and autonomous AI Radio DJ agent featuring real-time music curation, speech synthesis, and synchronized kinematics.

**Host / Hardware**: Reachy Mini robotic unit (Pollen Robotics architecture)  
**Integrations**: Spotify Connect, Multi-sink PulseAudio/ALSA routing, TTS voice pipeline  
**Session References**: Session 084 (xlerobot integration), Session 092 (Radio DJ multi-sink audio), Session 093 (Spotify Connect integration)

## Core Capabilities

1. **Autonomous Radio DJ**: Curates music tracks, injects contextual station announcements, track intros, and homelab news updates between songs.
2. **Multi-Sink Audio System**: Dual-channel sound architecture separating robotic voice output from high-fidelity music playback across local and networked audio sinks.
3. **Spotify Connect Integration**: Direct playback target on the local network enabling seamless remote control from mobile devices and AI agent triggers.
4. **Kinematic Expressiveness**: Synchronized head tilts, antenna gestures, and body sway responding dynamically to music BPM and speech intonation.
